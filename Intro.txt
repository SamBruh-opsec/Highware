HighASM:

Prog_start {start}
{start}:
    sys-end

NASM:

global _start
_start:
    mov al, 60h
    syscall

What we did:

Prog_start {start} ---------- global _start (Program's Start -> func. 'start')
{start}: ------------------- _start: (Calling the Program's kickstart function)
sys-end ----------------------- mov al, 60h 
                                                 syscall (Abstracting syscalls to just a single word)
